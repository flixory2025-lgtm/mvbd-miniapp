# MVBD SITE GUIDE (hand-written from the site's code)
Edit only when the HOW-TO steps change. Prices, plans, counts, links and new movies come automatically from `site-facts.md` and `news.md`.

## 1. What MVBD is
- MoviesVerseBD (MVBD) is a movie, series and anime platform for Bangladeshi viewers. Website: https://mvbds.xyz
- The website works like an app (MVBD Mini App). Content: Bangla, Hindi, English, Korean, South Indian (Hindi dubbed), Anime, series and more.
- Movies are opened and downloaded through Telegram (the MVBD cloud bot), not played directly inside the website.

## 2. Main areas of the app
- Bottom bar: Home, Anime, MeBook, Subscriptions, Profile.
- Menu (More): Settings, About Us, Modex.
- Home: search bar, trending, genre/category filters, upcoming releases, Top 20 famous.
- Anime: anime titles and anime upcoming list.
- MeBook: MVBD's community section (feed/posts, friends, chat).
- Modex: an app store section where users browse and install apps (search, categories, trending and new apps).
- Subscriptions: Free plan and Premium plans.
- Profile: account details, access status, referral code, notifications, support chat.
- Contact Us, About Us, Privacy Policy and Terms are linked from the footer/menu.

## 3. How to find a movie
1. Use the search bar at the top of Home. Type the movie or series name. Suggestions and your recent searches appear while typing.
2. Or browse by category (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Romance, Sci-Fi, Thriller, K-drama, Bangla, Marvel, DC, Netflix, Sports and more).
3. Tap a movie to open its page. You will see details, a trailer and the Download/Watch button.
- Tip: use the correct English name. Check the spelling on Google if the movie doesn't show up.
- Do not: don't add words like "download" or "link" to the name. Don't request a movie before searching.

## 4. How to watch or download
1. Open the movie page and tap the Telegram button ("Download on Telegram").
2. A popup shows two choices:
   - Download Normal (480p–720p): works on the Free plan.
   - Download 1080p–4K: needs an active Premium subscription.
3. Telegram opens the MVBD cloud bot and the file/link is delivered there. Install Telegram first if you don't have it.
- Trailer: tap the Trailer (YouTube) button on the movie page.
- If the movie doesn't open or you can't watch: see "Problems" below.

## 5. Movie not found / Movie request
1. First search the movie name in the Mini App/website search bar. Many users request movies that are already there.
2. If it is really not there, send the correct English name (check on Google) to the MVBD Request Group on Telegram: https://t.me/moviesversebdreq
3. The team checks and adds it if they can find it.
- Requests are not a guarantee. The movie may not be added, and no time is promised.
- Do not: don't request before searching, and don't write the name in a different spelling or only in Bangla.

## 6. Account and login
- Sign up with email + password, or "Continue with Google".
- Sign-up fields: full name, date of birth, and an optional referral code.
- A new account gets a 7-day free trial automatically.
- Each account is meant to be used on one device/browser at a time. Logging in somewhere else can log out the earlier session.
- Change password: Settings → Change Password.
- Do not: never share your password with anyone, including MVBD support or this AI.

## 7. Profile
- Shows your MVBD ID, name, date of birth (editable), your access status and when it expires.
- Also has: referral code, successful referrals count, Notifications, Support team chat, More settings.

## 8. Free plan and Premium
- Free: 480p–720p, free channels, MeBook, trailers. No 1080p–4K, no premium content, no download of premium quality.
- Premium plans (1, 2 or 3 months) give 1080p–4K, series and anime streaming, downloads on selected content, ad-free, premium content and priority support. Exact prices are in the SITE FACTS section.
### How to buy Premium
1. Open Profile or the Subscriptions tab and choose a plan.
2. The panel shows the amount and the Payment Number. Send exactly that amount to that number.
3. Enter your Transaction ID in the box and tap "Submit for Review".
4. An admin reviews it. Premium turns on after approval.
- Do not: don't pay to any number other than the one shown in the app. Don't submit a wrong or someone else's Transaction ID. Don't send your password with the payment.
- If approval is taking long, contact support with your MVBD ID and Transaction ID.
- The AI cannot see anyone's payment or Premium status.
